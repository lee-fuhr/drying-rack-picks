How we choose our picks

We don’t run our own lab tests or timed trials. Instead, we comb through months and years of owner reviews on major retail and forum sites, looking for consistent praise and recurring complaints. We also cross‑reference published evaluations from home‑goods reviewers who have tested racks over weeks. The focus is on what actually breaks: wobbly joints, rusted bars, sagging wires, or clips that snap. Beyond durability, we weigh practical user experience, how easily the rack opens and folds, whether it fits in a bathtub or closet, and how much drying space it truly provides. Value matters, so we compare each rack’s build and capacity against its price range, leaning toward picks that hold up well without costing a fortune.
